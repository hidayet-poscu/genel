# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Yumurta (Egg Timer) mobile application handles user data.

## 1. Data Controller

Application: Yumurta (Egg Timer)
Developer/Publisher: Eslima Software
Contact: eslimasoft6@gmail.com

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the Yumurta mobile application and related services used by the app.

## 3. Data We Collect

Yumurta is designed to work without creating an account.
The app does not directly request or store personal information such as name, email, phone number, or address within the app flow.

However, the following data may be processed by third-party service providers used by the app:

- Device and app metadata (for example app version, operating system, device model)
- Advertising identifiers and ad interaction signals
- Basic diagnostic or crash information

## 4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party SDKs/services, including:

- Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Notifications

Yumurta may use local notifications for timer-related reminders.
These notifications are generated on-device and are not used to track users.

## 6. How We Use Data

Data processed via third-party services may be used for:

- Showing ads in the application
- Measuring ad performance and preventing fraud
- Improving app stability and reliability

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal data.
Data may be shared with third-party providers only as required to deliver app functionality (for example ad serving and technical diagnostics).

## 8. Data Retention

Yumurta does not maintain a user account database.
Any retention periods for data collected by third-party SDKs are governed by those providers' policies.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children under 13.
If you believe a child has provided personal data through third-party services, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

## 10. International Transfers

Third-party providers may process data on servers located in different countries.
Such processing is subject to the provider's legal safeguards.

## 11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights such as access, deletion, objection, or restriction regarding personal data processed by third parties.
For requests, contact us and/or the relevant third-party provider.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Updated versions will be published with a revised "Last updated" date.
